# Sign Labels

Give your arrow signs a label. The text sign lets you write on it — the
placeable arrow, the skull and brute signs, both warning signs and both wooden
signs don't, so marking a waypoint meant planting a text sign next to the
arrow. With this mod, interacting with any of those seven signs opens the
game's own sign window, exactly like on a text sign: text, visibility toggle,
parental filter and controller support included.

## Default visibility

Every sign keeps its own visibility — Off, while targeted, or Always — just like
the vanilla text sign. A **Default visibility** option (Options → Mod settings →
Sign Labels) sets what a sign you place starts at, default Hover. Signs loaded
from a save keep their saved state.

## Good to know

Needs CoreLib and Mod Settings Menu, on a server as well. Mining a labelled
sign always drops exactly one. The three signs with a vanilla label are
untouched, and holoboards are not covered.
